Maker Garage Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Maker Garage respects your privacy. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when you use the Maker Garage app and related services.

Information We Collect

We may collect account information such as your name, email address, username, profile information, and content you choose to publish. For purchases and sales, we may process order, shipping, transaction, and payout-related information.

Payments

Payment processing may be handled by third-party payment providers such as Stripe and Apple. Maker Garage does not store full payment card numbers.

How We Use Information

We use information to operate the app, provide marketplace features, process orders, support users, prevent abuse, improve performance, and comply with legal obligations.

Public Content

Projects, comments, profile information, images, videos, and other content that you choose to publish may be visible to other users.

Service Providers

We may use trusted service providers for hosting, analytics, authentication, payments, storage, notifications, and related infrastructure. These providers may process data only as needed to provide their services.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to operate the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

Account Deletion

You may request account deletion through the app where available. Certain records may be retained when required for legal, fraud-prevention, tax, payment, or dispute-resolution purposes.

Children

Maker Garage is not intended for children under the minimum age required by applicable law for independent use of the service.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of Maker Garage after an update means the revised policy will apply from its effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact maker9garage@gmail.com.
