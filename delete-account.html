Delete Your Maker Garage Account

Last updated: November 2026

This page explains how to delete your account in the Maker Garage app, published by Brandloom LLC, and what happens to your data when you do.

How to Delete Your Account

In the app (iPhone or Android)

  1. Open Maker Garage and sign in.
  2. Go to your Profile and open Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

On the website

Sign in at web.makergarage.app, open Settings and choose Delete account.

By email

If you can't sign in, email maker9garage@gmail.com from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete the account and confirm by email.

Deleting your account in the app or on the website takes effect right away and cannot be undone.

What Is Deleted

  • Your account, sign-in details, username and profile.
  • Your projects, with their descriptions, and the likes, comments and saves on them.
  • Your comments, likes, saves, follows, blocks and notifications.
  • Your requests and proposals.
  • Your chats and support conversations.
  • Your Maker Credits balance, credit history and unlocked digital guides.
  • The links that send notifications to your devices, and all signed-in sessions.
  • Reports you made about other users or content.

Photos and videos you uploaded are removed from your projects and profile and are no longer shown in the app. Copies of the files may remain with our media storage provider; email us if you want them removed too.

What Is Kept

  • Records of orders, payments, payouts and sales, which we must keep for tax, accounting, refunds and disputes. Your name, contact details and shipping address are removed from them.
  • Reports other people made about your account or content, kept for safety and moderation without your identity.

These records are kept only as long as the law requires for those purposes.

Deleting Some Data Without Deleting Your Account

You can delete your projects, comments and requests in the app at any time. For anything else, email maker9garage@gmail.com.
