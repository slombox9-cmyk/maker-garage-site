Frequently asked questions

How do I contact Maker Garage support?

Use the Email Support button. Include a short description of your issue. Never send passwords or complete payment card numbers.

Where can I check my order status?

Open Maker Garage, go to Orders, and select the order to view its current status.

How do seller payouts work?

Eligible sellers complete Stripe onboarding. Payout timing depends on order status, delivery, buyer protection, and any open return, report, or dispute.

How do I delete my account?

Use the account deletion option in Maker Garage settings. If you cannot access your account, contact support.

Still need help?

Contact Maker Garage support and include the order ID when your question is order-related.
