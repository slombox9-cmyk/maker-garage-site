Maker Garage

A marketplace where makers can publish, discover, buy, and sell creative projects, digital files, services, and custom project requests.

About Maker Garage

Maker Garage connects creators, builders, and customers interested in DIY projects, robotics, CNC projects, digital plans, custom builds, and other creative work.

The Maker Garage mobile application is currently under development.

How the Marketplace Works

Sellers can publish projects, digital products, services, and custom project offers. Customers can discover listings, communicate with sellers, and make purchases through the platform.

Payments are securely processed through Stripe. Maker Garage may charge a platform service fee on completed transactions.

Refund and Dispute Policy

Customers should contact Maker Garage support if an order is not delivered, is significantly different from its description, or has another transaction-related problem.

Refund eligibility depends on the type of product or service, the order status, and the circumstances of the transaction. Digital products that have already been downloaded may not be refundable except where required by law.

Privacy Policy

Maker Garage may collect information needed to operate the marketplace, including account information, transaction details, communications, and technical information.

Payment card information is processed by Stripe and is not stored directly by Maker Garage. Personal information is used to provide, secure, and improve the service and to comply with legal obligations.

Terms of Service

Users must provide accurate information and may not use Maker Garage for illegal, fraudulent, deceptive, dangerous, or prohibited transactions.

Sellers are responsible for accurately describing and delivering their products or services. Maker Garage may review, suspend, or remove accounts and listings that violate platform rules or applicable law.
